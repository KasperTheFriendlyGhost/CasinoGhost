CasinoGhost

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Applies to the CasinoGhost app for iPhone and iPad and to the web version.

In short: CasinoGhost collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

To remember your settings and pick up where you left off, CasinoGhost saves the following on your device only:

  • your table rules, side-bet paytables and table limits;
  • your dashboard layout and preferences (sound, haptics, appearance);
  • your practice session: practice bankroll, statistics, recent decisions and the shoe in play.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Like any app's data, it can be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of the device, which Apple's terms cover. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Data → Erase all data in the app, or by deleting the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It does not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking services.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other permission.
  • It makes no network requests of its own. Fonts, card art and code are built into the app, and it works fully offline.

Links to other websites

The app links to a few outside pages, such as gambling help lines, open-source credits and this policy. They open in Safari or an in-app Safari view, and those sites' own privacy policies apply to them.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to operate and secure its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The web version stores its data in your browser's local storage, on your device, and can be cleared from Settings or from your browser.

Children

CasinoGhost simulates casino blackjack. It is rated for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If the app ever begins collecting data, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/KasperTheFriendlyGhost/CasinoGhost/issues, or see the support page.

CasinoGhost is a training tool. No real money is wagered.
